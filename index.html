The Routine Issue

Six routines that run the week

Short rituals, each one done on a page of this app. Open an article to read how.

Morning · 5 minChoose the day before it chooses youCheck energy and mood, then pick one big, up to three medium and up to five small tasks.

Start on Today. The energy and mood scale tells you how heavy the day should be. A low score keeps the day light without forbidding anything. Then fill the slots: one big task, up to three medium, up to five small. The ring at the top shows the time you have spent.

During the dayWrite it down, time it, finish itEvery task gets a duration and a priority. Keep at most two in progress.

On Projects › Tasks, write each task as it comes. Give it a duration in minutes and a priority from the Eisenhower matrix. Start the timer on the task you are doing. The Kanban rules at the top of the page keep you to two tasks in progress. A blocked task keeps its reason.

OrganiseA project is a sentence with a next stepGroup tasks into projects and sections, and name one next action of 30 minutes or less.

On Projects › Sections and Projects, each project holds sections, and each section holds tasks. The progress block shows what is done and what is left. A next action starts with a verb, so you know where to begin.

CalendarProtect the hours that matterReserve a slot for each type of work, and treat it like an appointment.

On Calendar, click a day to see its hours. Draw a block for each kind of work and drop a task onto it. The time-blocking method is explained at the bottom of the page.

Evening · 5 minThree lines, then close the dayWhat got done, what is left, one thing you are grateful for.

At the end of Today, open the end-of-day ritual. Write the three lines, then call it a day. The day is saved, and it appears in your calendar and your review.

Friday · 20 minLook back, then plan the next weekFive review questions, the Eisenhower matrix for next week, and a note in the journal.

On Review, answer the five questions, prepare the week in the Eisenhower matrix, and write a line in the journal. Export a backup while you are there.
